# Scholarship Poster Studio 🎓

A visual poster generator designed for scholarship announcements, updates, and educational marketing. Created from the 4 visual reference styles with live typography, color schemes, and instant high-resolution exports.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Four Signature Reference Themes**:
   - **Modern Teal**: Clean wave accents, dark cyan branding, and professional layout.
   - **Lavender Dream**: Pastel purple aesthetic with handwritten callouts (*"Your Potential Matters"*).
   - **Editorial Beige**: Classic serif typography, sage tones, and an academic look.
   - **Bold Navy & Yellow**: High-contrast modern look with bright yellow accents and energetic callouts (*"Apply Now! ⚡"*).

2. **Preset Quick-Fill Library**:
   - Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship (₹ 2,00,000)
   - HDFC Badhte Kadam Scholarship (₹ 1,00,000)
   - Tata Trust Merit & Means Scholarship (₹ 50,000 / yr)
   - AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls (₹ 50,000 / yr)
   - Fulbright-Nehru Fellowships (USA - Fully Funded)
   - Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (UK - Fully Funded)

3. **Multi-Format Canvas Sizing**:
   - **4:5 Portrait** (1080 × 1350 px) — Ideal for Instagram Feeds, LinkedIn Posts, and WhatsApp flyers.
   - **1:1 Square** (1080 × 1080 px) — Perfect for standard square posts.
   - **9:16 Story / Status** (1080 × 1920 px) — Tailored for Instagram Stories, WhatsApp Status, and YouTube Shorts.

4. **Multi-Format Export Options**:
   - **Ultra HD PNG**: Supersampled 3x render for crystal-clear text on any display.
   - **Print-Ready PDF**: Vector-friendly single-page document for printouts or physical noticeboards.
   - **1-Click Clipboard Copy**: Instantly paste your generated poster into Slack, WhatsApp Web, or Canva.

5. **Customizable Artwork & Branding**:
   - Built-in 3D/vector illustrations: Graduation Cap & Book Stack, Tied Diploma Roll, Classic Arch Window.
   - Custom image upload support for official scholarship logos or university banners.
   - Brand name and slogan customization (defaulting to *"ScholaRise - Learn • Grow • Achieve"*).
